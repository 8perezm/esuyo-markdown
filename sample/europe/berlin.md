# 🏗️ Berlin, Germany

**Population:** 3.9 million  
**Language:** German  
**Currency:** Euro (€)

---

## 🇩🇪 About Berlin

Berlin is Germany's capital and a city that has **reinvented itself twice** in a single lifetime — imperial, divided, and reunited. It's a byword for experimentation, from Bauhaus architecture to techno clubs in former power stations.

> "Berlin is poor, but sexy." — *Klaus Wowereit*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Era | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Brandenburg Gate | 1791 | Symbol of German reunification |
| Reichstag Dome | 1999 | Glass cupola by Norman Foster |
| Berlin Wall Memorial | 1961–1989 | Open-air memorial on Bernauer Straße |
| Museum Island | 1830–1930 | UNESCO site, five museums |
| Fernsehturm | 1969 | 368 m TV tower, views over the city |

## 🎨 Art & Culture

- **Bauhaus Archive** — The movement's founding collection
- **East Side Gallery** — 1.3 km of murals on a surviving Wall section
- **Kreuzberg** — Street art, markets, and late-night food
- **Berghain** — Infamous techno club in a former power plant

## 🥨 Food to Try

1. **Currywurst** — Sausage, curry ketchup, and fries
2. **Pretzel** (*Brezel*) — Lye-baked, salty, essential
3. **Döner Kebab** — Berlin's late-night staple
4. **Apfelstrudel** — Warm apple strudel with custard

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. The Wall fell on **9 November 1989**
2. Berlin is **twice the size** of Paris but has fewer residents
3. The city has over **170 museums**
4. Berlin's U-Bahn opened in **1902**
5. There are **~1,000 bridges** — more than Venice
