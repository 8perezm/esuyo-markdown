# ☀️ Madrid, Spain

**Population:** 3.3 million (metro: 6.7 million)  
**Language:** Spanish  
**Currency:** Euro (€)

---

## 🇪🇸 About Madrid

Madrid sits on the **high plateau of the Meseta**, 657 m above sea level — the highest capital in Europe. Its golden-hour light has inspired Velázquez, Goya, and generations of filmmakers.

> "Madrid is a city to be enjoyed, slowly and without hurry." — *Popular saying*

> [!NOTE]
> Madrid's official city motto is *"Madrid, Madrid, Madrid"* — three times, because *Cervantes, Velázquez, and Goya* all lived here.

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Built | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Royal Palace | 1755 | 3,418 rooms — Europe's largest palace |
| Prado Museum | 1819 | Velázquez, Goya, El Greco |
| Plaza Mayor | 1619 | Baroque square, once a bullring |
| Retiro Park | 1631 | 125 ha, Crystal Palace & rowboats |
| Gran Vía | 1910 | The "Broadway of Madrid" |

## 🍮 The Art of the *Sobremesa*

- **Tapas** — Small plates: *patatas bravas*, *jamón ibérico*, *gambas al ajillo*
- **Churros con chocolate** — Thick hot chocolate for dunking
- **Cocido madrileño** — Hearty chickpea stew
- **Tarta de Santiago** — Almond cake from Galicia

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. The **Prado** holds over 8,000 paintings
2. Madrid has **three Michelin-starred** restaurants per 100k residents
3. The Metro opened in **1919** and has 302 stations
4. Spain's **Royal Family** still lives in the Royal Palace's private wing
5. The city's **Plaza Mayor** hosted the Spanish Inquisition's autos-da-fé
