# 🎻 Vienna, Austria

**Population:** 2 million (metro: 2.8 million)  
**Language:** German (Austrian variety)  
**Currency:** Euro (€)

---

## 🎼 About Vienna

For a thousand years the seat of the **Habsburg Empire**, Vienna is a city of imperial palaces, coffee houses, and an almost absurd musical pedigree — Mozart, Beethoven, Schubert, and Strauss all called it home.

> "Vienna is not a city. It is a state of mind." — *Anonymous*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

- **Schönbrunn Palace** — 1,441 rooms, the Habsburg summer residence
- **Hofburg** — The imperial palace complex, now the president's office
- **St. Stephen's Cathedral** — Gothic spires in the city centre
- **Belvedere** — Baroque palace housing Klimt's *The Kiss*
- **Prater** — amusement park with the giant **Wiener Riesenrad** wheel

## ☕ Coffee House Culture

```
☕ Melange     — espresso + steamed milk + foam
☕ Einspänner  — black coffee topped with whipped cream
☕ Franziskaner — coffee with milk and a dollop of cream
🍰 Sachertorte  — chocolate cake with apricot jam
🍰 Apfelstrudel — flaky pastry, apples, cinnamon
```

> [!TIP]
> Viennese coffee houses are **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage**. Order a coffee and stay for hours — nobody will rush you.

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. Vienna was voted the **world's most liveable city** for years running
2. The **Vienna Boys' Choir** dates back to 1498
3. **Sigmund Freud** lived and worked here
4. The **Prater** wheel starred in *The Third Man*
5. Vienna has over **100 museums**
