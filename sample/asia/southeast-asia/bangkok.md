# 🛕 Bangkok, Thailand

**Population:** 10.5 million (metro: 17 million)  
**Language:** Thai  
**Currency:** Thai Baht (฿)

---

## 🛶 About Bangkok

Bangkok — *"City of Angels"* — is Thailand's capital and a riot of gold-leaf temples, floating markets, and neon nightlife. Its official Thai name is one of the **longest place names in the world**.

> "Bangkok is a city of contradictions — serene and chaotic, ancient and relentless." — *Anonymous*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

- **Grand Palace** — 2.19 km² of glittering royal buildings
- **Wat Pho** — Home of the 46 m **Reclining Buddha**
- **Wat Arun** — Riverside temple with porcelain mosaics
- **Chatuchak Market** — 15,000 stalls, one of the world's largest
- **Canal tours** — The "Venice of the East" along Thonburi's khlongs

## 🍲 Food to Try

1. **Pad Thai** 🍜 — Tamarind-sour stir-fried noodles
2. **Tom Yum Goong** 🦐 — Hot & sour prawn soup
3. **Som Tam** 🥗 — Green papaya salad, fiercely spicy
4. **Massaman Curry** 🍛 — Rich, mild, Muslim-influenced
5. **Mango Sticky Rice** 🥭 — The perfect finish

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. The full Thai name translates to roughly *"City of angels, great city of immortals..."*
2. Bangkok has over **400 Buddhist temples**
3. The city sits on the **Chao Phraya River**
4. **Songkran** (Thai New Year) is celebrated with country-wide water fights in April
5. It's one of the hottest capitals — routinely **35°C+** in April
