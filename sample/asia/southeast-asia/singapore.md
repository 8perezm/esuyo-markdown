# 🌆 Singapore

**Population:** 5.9 million  
**Language:** English, Malay, Mandarin, Tamil  
**Currency:** Singapore Dollar (S$)

---

## 🦁 About Singapore

A **city-state** of just 735 km², Singapore transformed from a British trading post into one of the world's most prosperous and greenest cities — a "garden city" where skyscrapers and supertrees share the skyline.

> "Singapore is a place where the future is always under construction." — *Anonymous*

## 🍜 Hawker Culture

> [!TIP]
> Singapore's **hawker centres** are on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list. Look for the MICHELIN Bib Gourmand stalls in old food courts.

| Dish | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Hainanese Chicken Rice | Poached chicken, fragrant rice, chilli sauce |
| Laksa | Spicy coconut noodle soup |
| Char Kway Teow | Stir-fried flat noodles, cockles, wok hei |
| Chilli Crab | Crab in sweet-spicy tomato sauce |
| Kaya Toast | Coconut jam toast with soft-boiled eggs |

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. Singapore became independent in **1965**
2. It has **no natural disasters** — outside the typhoon belt
3. **~90%** of its food is imported
4. The island runs on a **congestion pricing** system since 1975
5. Changi Airport is regularly voted the **world's best**
