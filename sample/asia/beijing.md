# 🧱 Beijing, China

**Population:** 21.5 million (municipality)  
**Language:** Mandarin Chinese  
**Currency:** Chinese Yuan (¥ / CNY)

---

## 🐉 About Beijing

Beijing is the **cultural and political heart of China**, a city where imperial history and futuristic development sit side by side. It has been a capital on and off for over **800 years**.

> "He who has not been to the Great Wall is not a true man." — *Chinese proverb*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Built | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Forbidden City | 1420 | 9,999 rooms, imperial palace for 500 years |
| Great Wall (Mutianyu) | 7th C BC onward | UNESCO site, best-preserved sections |
| Temple of Heaven | 1420 | Imperial ceremonies for good harvests |
| Summer Palace | 1750 | 290 ha of lakes and gardens |
| Tiananmen Square | 1958 | One of the world's largest public squares |

## 🧱 The Great Wall

- Built across **2,000+ years** by successive dynasties
- Total length: **21,196 km** (Ming sections ~8,850 km)
- Visible from space? **Mostly a myth** — hard to see with the naked eye
- Best nearby access: **Mutianyu** and **Jinshanling**

## 🥡 Food to Try

1. **Peking Duck** 🍗 — Crispy skin, pancakes, hoisin sauce
2. **Zhajiangmian** — Noodles with fermented bean sauce
3. **Jiaozi** 🥟 — Dumplings, especially at New Year
4. **Tanghulu** — Candied hawthorn skewers
5. **Douhua** — Silken tofu pudding, sweet or savoury

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. Beijing hosted the **2008 Summer** and **2022 Winter** Olympics — first city to host both
2. The Forbidden City's layout follows strict **imperial symmetry**
3. The city has **23 UNESCO sites** in and around it
4. Beijing's hutong alleys are being preserved as living history
5. The **Bird's Nest** stadium seats 80,000
