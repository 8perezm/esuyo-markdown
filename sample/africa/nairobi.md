# 🦁 Nairobi, Kenya

**Population:** 5.1 million  
**Languages:** Swahili & English  
**Currency:** Kenyan Shilling (KSh)

---

## 🌍 About Nairobi

Nairobi is East Africa's largest city and a rare thing: a **capital with a national park inside its city limits**. Skyscrapers give way to savannah less than 20 minutes from the centre.

> "Nairobi is where the city meets the wild." — *Anonymous*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Opened | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Nairobi National Park | 1946 | 117 km² of protected savannah, 10 km from downtown |
| David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust | 1977 | Orphaned elephant & rhino nursery |
| Giraffe Centre | 1983 | Feed endangered giraffes eye-to-eye |
| Karen Blixen Museum | 1930s | *Out of Africa* author's former home |
| Bomas of Kenya | 1971 | Traditional homesteads & dance shows |

## 🦓 Wildlife in the City

- **Lions, leopards & cheetahs** — yes, within the national park
- **Black rhinos** — one of the most successful conservation stories
- **Giraffes** — the endangered Rothschild's giraffe at the Giraffe Centre
- **Over 400 bird species** in the park alone

> [!NOTE]
> Nairobi National Park is fenced on **three sides only** — animals can migrate south into the Kitengela conservancy.

## 🍲 Food to Try

1. **Ugali** — Maize flour porridge, the staple
2. **Nyama Choma** 🔥 — Grilled meat, best shared
3. **Sukuma Wiki** — Collard greens with onions & tomatoes
4. **Mandazi** 🍩 — Sweet fried dough, perfect with tea
5. **Chai** ☕ — Kenyan tea is a world exporter

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. Nairobi was founded in **1899** as a railway depot
2. It became Kenya's capital in **1963**, at independence
3. Sits at **1,795 m** — one of Africa's highest capitals
4. The city is named after the **Nairobi River**
5. It's a major hub for **UN agencies** — home to UN Environment
