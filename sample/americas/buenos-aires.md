# 💃 Buenos Aires, Argentina

**Population:** 3.1 million (metro: 15 million)  
**Language:** Spanish (Rioplatense)  
**Currency:** Argentine Peso ($)

---

## 🇦🇷 About Buenos Aires

Often called the **"Paris of South America"**, Buenos Aires is a city of grand European boulevards, tango halls, and intense political history — shaped by waves of immigration from Italy and Spain.

> "Buenos Aires is a city that looks at itself in the mirror and likes what it sees." — *Jorge Luis Borges (paraphrased)*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Built | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Casa Rosada | 1898 | Pink presidential palace on Plaza de Mayo |
| Obelisco | 1936 | 67 m monument at the Avenida 9 de Julio |
| Teatro Colón | 1908 | One of the world's great opera houses |
| La Recoleta Cemetery | 1822 | Eva Perón's resting place |
| San Telmo | 16th C | Tango, antiques, Sunday market |

## 💃 Tango

```
🪗 Bandoneón  — the instrument that defines the sound
💃 Milonga    — a tango social dance party
🎵 "Libertango" — Astor Piazzolla's masterpiece
🏛️ Caminito   — colourful street in La Boca, tango's birthplace
```

> [!TIP]
> A *milonga* is not a show — it's a **social dance** with a strict code of etiquette (*cabeceo*, the silent invitation to dance).

## 🥩 Food to Try

1. **Asado** 🥩 — barbecue, a national ritual
2. **Empanadas** 🥟 — baked or fried filled pastries
3. **Milanesa** — breaded cutlet, often served with lemon
4. **Dulce de leche** — caramelised milk in everything
5. **Malbec wine** 🍷 — Argentina's flagship varietal

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. **Avenida 9 de Julio** is among the widest streets in the world
2. Buenos Aires has **over 130 libraries** and a huge book culture
3. The city is home to the **world's first subway** in Latin America (1913)
4. **Eva Perón** lies in Recoleta Cemetery
5. Argentina gave the world the **tango** and **Messi**
