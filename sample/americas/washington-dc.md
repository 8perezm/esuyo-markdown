# 🏛️ Washington, D.C.

**Population:** 689,000 (metro: 6.4 million)  
**Language:** English  
**Currency:** US Dollar ($)

---

## 🇺🇸 About Washington, D.C.

Unlike most capitals, Washington was **purpose-built** as a compromise seat of government between North and South. Pierre L'Enfant's 1791 plan laid out broad avenues, ceremonial circles, and a strict height limit that still shapes the skyline.

> "Washington is a city of monuments built by a nation that could not agree on much." — *Anonymous*

## 🏛️ Must-See Landmarks

| Landmark | Completed | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Lincoln Memorial | 1922 | 5.8 m seated statue, MLK's "Dream" speech |
| Washington Monument | 1884 | 169 m obelisk, tallest structure in the district |
| U.S. Capitol | 1800 | Neoclassical seat of Congress |
| White House | 1800 | Official residence since John Adams |
| Jefferson Memorial | 1943 | Dome overlooking the Tidal Basin |

## 🗂️ The Smithsonian

> [!NOTE]
> The Smithsonian is the **world's largest museum, education, and research complex** — 19 museums and 9 research centres, mostly free to enter.

- **National Air and Space Museum** ✈️ — the Wright Flyer, Apollo 11 command module
- **National Museum of Natural History** 🦖 — the Hope Diamond
- **National Gallery of Art** 🎨 — Leonardo, Van Gogh, Picasso
- **National Museum of African American History & Culture**

## 🌸 Cherry Blossoms

Every spring, **3,000+ cherry trees** gifted by Japan in 1912 ring the Tidal Basin — the focus of a two-week national festival. 🌸

## 📅 Quick Facts

1. D.C. is a **federal district**, not part of any state
2. Residents gained the right to vote for president in **1961** (23rd Amendment)
3. The **Height of Buildings Act** limits most buildings to 45 m
4. The Capitol dome is **88 m** tall
5. There are **over 170 foreign embassies** along Embassy Row
